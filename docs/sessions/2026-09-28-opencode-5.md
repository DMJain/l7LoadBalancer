ADR-writing session, not pre-baked in the spec. Asked the owner to choose. The
owner chose **Docker container**: the digest-pinned distroless image is the
packaged deployment artifact, with the bare binary as the supported primitive
beneath it and Kubernetes explicitly not the target (no clustering, no external
state, no scale-out; adopting it would imply operational readiness ADR-0005
disclaims). The ADR still records all three options and the reasons each
other one was rejected.
