## Decisions

- **No ADR.** Docs-only, no design decision beyond the owner's scenario list
  (already recorded in ADR-0023 / the bundle spec).
- **Full six-scenario list** chosen by the owner at ticket start.
- **The script carries a *Rehearsal record* and a findings list**, per the
  ticket's "observed state … fixed in the script (or recorded as a finding), not
  left aspirational". Four findings: the p2c 50/33/17/0 split needs four
  *distinct* estimates (with one slow backend the three fast ones tie and split
  evenly, so the script adds an explicit staggered-latency step); the generator
  delivers ~370 of 400 req/s and falls off above ~800 (timer granularity); a
  removed backend's `/stats` keeps ticking because the *other* LBs probe it; no
  `wget` in the distroless backend image.
- **Spec-vs-reality reconciliation:** ticket item 5 / spec US67 assume the demo
  rate keeps least-connections' in-flight counts *above zero*; rehearsal showed
  0–1 at 400 req/s (a request lasts ~2.5 ms) and still ~0 at 800, so the
  tie-break dominates. Recorded as the script's least-conn finding rather than
  promising a spread-out split. Changing the stack's default rate is T16.2/T16.3.2
  scope, not T17.1.
