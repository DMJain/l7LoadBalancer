## Decisions

- **Full exponential wait, no cap** (review finding). A `maxSleep` cap that
  resampled inflated the effective rate at low configured rates (as r → 0 it
  approached 1/s), contradicting the open-loop Poisson requirement. The wait is
  honoured in full; only `maxWaitSeconds` guards `time.Duration` overflow. A
  pending arrival scheduled at the old rate still fires; a rate change affects
  arrivals scheduled after it.
- **`newGenerator` is the single domain-validation layer.** `configFromEnv`
  parses and names the variable on a bad value; `newGenerator` checks rank
  bounds, allowlist membership, and the rate/in-flight bounds. This removes the
  duplicated checks the standards review flagged. The env test mirrors `main`:
  parse, then construct.
- **`-addr` flag for the control port**, not env: the dummy backend sets its
  listen address with `-addr`, and the spec's env list is the generator's
  configuration (rank, share, rate, target, allowlist, bound), not its port.
- **`errors` counts transport failures only** — a response of any status is
  `completed`; a flaky backend's 5xx is visible in Grafana, not in this counter.
- **Empty POST body is a 200 no-op** (all fields omitted), the T16.1 precedent.
- **A new ADR is warranted** (vs T16.1's "no ADR"): the spec fixed the
  generator's *behaviour* but left its scheduling law, drop semantics and
  defaults to the implementation, and those belong in a durable record rather
  than only in `.scratch/`.
