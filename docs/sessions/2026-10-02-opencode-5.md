## Done

- Claimed S5.T16.3.2 (`1e0cfd7`).
- `demo/docker-compose.yml` (project `l7loadbalancer-demo`): four LBs built from
  the repo-root `Dockerfile` (`lb-roundrobin`, `lb-leastconn`,
  `lb-consistent-hash`, `lb-p2c-ewma`), each bind-mounting its own config and
  sharing `backend1`–`backend4`; four backends with `ADMIN_ENABLED=true`
  (internal `:9091`) and `LOG_REQUESTS=false`; eight generators `gen1`–`gen8`
  from `x-generator`/`x-generator-env` anchors with `RANK=1..8`,
  `TOTAL_RATE=400`, `CLIENTS=8`, `ZIPF_S=1.0`, the four LB URLs as
  `TARGET_ALLOWLIST`, and `GOMEMLIMIT=256MiB`/`GOGC=50`; a 1 s Prometheus with
  one job per LB; a Grafana with `GF_SECURITY_ALLOW_EMBEDDING=true` and
  anonymous Viewer mounting the shared provisioning and dashboard read-only.
- Backends deliberately have **no** restart policy: `restart: unless-stopped`
  would resurrect a `docker kill`, defeating the T16.4.2 revive semantics.
- Published ports are only `127.0.0.1:3000` (Grafana) and
  `127.0.0.1:8080/8082/8083/8084` (the four LB client ports); everything else
  stays on the internal demo network.
- `demo/README.md` documents the one-command up/down, the published surface, the
  `var-window=15s` dashboard URL, the traffic defaults, and the independence from
  the root and bench stacks.
- `Makefile`: `demo-up` (`docker compose -f demo/docker-compose.yml up -d
  --build`) and `demo-down`.
