## Findings for S5.T17

- P2C-EWMA is rank-based (`internal/balancer/p2c_ewma.go`): with 4 backends the
  shares are about 50/33/17/0 whatever the latency gap, and with no EWMA decay
  the slowest backend can stay starved after its latency is restored.
- least_conn's first-in-order tie-break concentrates low-rate traffic on
  backend1, so the demo rate must keep in-flight counts above zero.
