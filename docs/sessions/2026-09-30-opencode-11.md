## Decisions

- Refusal lives in `bench/repro.sh`, not in `bench/run.sh`. The harness stays a
  development tool that records `git_dirty`/running containers so uncommitted
  work can be smoke-tested (spec §52, §219); enforcement sits at the publication
  boundary. The two scripts cannot share code because `run.sh` executes `main`
  at source time, so its `ROOT`/`COMPOSE` plumbing is restated (accepted).
- The vCPU floor is a constant (`MIN_VCPUS=8`) matching the fixed 8-vCPU split;
  raising it locally is how the refusal is exercised, as the ticket requires.
- The dirty-tree check is the literal ticket reading — a bare `git status
  --porcelain`, no `bench/results` exclusion — so a published run traces to a
  commit. Gitignored artifacts (`certs/`, `bench/results/.tmp/`) are not
  untracked and do not count. A comment records the intent.
- No ADR.
