## Done

  loud-failure convention: `os.LookupEnv`, unset or empty → `def`, and only the

## Decisions

- **Exact-string parsing, not `strconv.ParseBool`.** `ParseBool` also accepts
  `1`/`0`/`t`/`f`/mixed case; the ticket says only `true`/`false` are accepted
  and "any other value" errors, so plain `switch` on the raw value is the
  literal contract. `TRUE` is deliberately an error and locked by a test.
- **The gate lives in `logRequest`, not at each call site.** One early return is
  the smallest change that guarantees every path (including future ones) is
  silenced. A per-request closure was written first and replaced during refactor:
  it allocated on the exact hot path this ticket exists to protect, and a guard
  inside the shared logger is just as central.
- **Bench anchor only.** All four backends share `x-backend`, so one line sets
  the whole bench rig; the demo stack keeps its default.
