## What happened

### S5.T6, attempt 1

Claimed S5.T6 (`f59a23f`), cloned `main` to a scratch dir, launched
`make bench-repro` detached. Preflight and the 10-combination smoke passed; the
full matrix ran to `run 11/71` (~30 min) and then died at **run 19/71
`h2/leastconn/1mb/lb peak-search`** with `make` exit 137.

### Diagnosis

Docker Desktop's own log and the VM kernel log made the cause unambiguous: the
Docker VM was OOM-killing **`vegeta`** (anon-RSS ~5 GB against the 8.2 GB VM).
The host swap was also nearly full. Attempt 2 (relaunched under `tmux` +
`caffeinate` to rule out tool-session churn) died identically at **run 17/71**, so
it was deterministic, not transient.

### S5.T6.1, round 1 (heap bound) — insufficient

Owner approved bounding the generator's Go heap (`GOMAXPROCS=2`,
`GOMEMLIMIT=3GiB`, `GOGC=50`; ADR-0021). Verified in isolation that vegeta
stayed ≤ 3.9 GB, but re-running the real `discover_peak` for `leastconn/1mb/lb`
still OOM-killed the VM — now the **LB** (`oom=true`, exit 137, 3.0 GB) as well
as vegeta (3.69 GB). Bounding the heap only moved the victim. Reported back.

### S5.T6.1, round 2 (worker cap + delivery guard + per-size seeds)

Owner expanded the scope. Root cause: vegeta's `-max-workers` defaults to
**unbounded**, so at 1000 req/s against the slow 1 MB path it spawns thousands
of concurrent in-flight requests, the LB's per-stream buffers grow, and the two
together exceed the VM. A worker cap fixed the memory (vegeta 203 MiB, LB
82 MiB) but made vegeta **silently under-send** (3,745 requests in 20 s against a
1000/s target) — p99 read 27 ms, which would have become a **false peak**. The
rig's real 1 MB ceiling is ~200 req/s (a 200/s target delivered exactly
4000/4000), so the 1000/s seed was itself the trigger.

Implemented exactly the owner's combined scope (see commits). The pre-fix
`leastconn/10kb/lb` "peak=8000/s" was proven to be a **false peak**: its own
committed measured report shows throughput **388.74 req/s**, success **21.5%**,
p99 **30 s**. The delivery guard is precisely what catches that class of number.

## Done

- `bench/run.sh`:
  - `-max-workers`/`-max-connections` (both 1024) on all four `vegeta attack`
    invocations;
  - `passes <target>` also requires delivered ≥ `DELIVERY_MIN_RATIO_PCT` (95%)
    of target, measured as the request count over the window, and sets
    `STEP_FAIL_REASON`;
  - per-size `seed_rate_for`/`max_rate_for`/`granularity_for` (case-based, so
    macOS bash 3.2 still runs the harness): 1 MB `100/500/25`, 200 B & 10 KB
    `1000/200000/500`;
  - `discover_peak <url> <size>` sets `PEAK_RATE`/`PEAK_SEED`/`PEAK_FAIL_REASON`
    as globals instead of echoing (a `$(...)` call runs it in a subshell and
    discarded the reason — caught during verification and fixed);
  - `run_scenario` reports `under-delivered; harness-limited; not a peak` on an
    under-delivered skip, `peak=${peak}/s (sustained)` and
    `peak_status=sustained` on a real peak.
- `bench/docker-compose.yml`: keeps the ADR-0021 defensive heap cap.
- `bench/README.md`: *Generator bounds and honest peak discovery* documents the
