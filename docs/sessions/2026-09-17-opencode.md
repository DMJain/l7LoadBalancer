## Decisions
- **Why `tools.go`?** S1.T1's acceptance requires both "`go.mod` requires
  the deps" and "`go mod tidy` produces no further diff", but `go mod tidy`
  prunes requirements no package imports — with no consumer yet, it deletes
  both. Verified on Go 1.25.1. A build-tagged blank-import file is the
  standard way to pin a dependency ahead of its first import. Recorded in
  ADR-0003 because it deviates from `spec.md`'s literal "only
  `go.mod`/`go.sum`" wording; `docs/design/sprint-1-contracts.md` requires
  deviations to be ADR'd rather than made silently.
