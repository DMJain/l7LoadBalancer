## Decisions

- **Accepted the S5.T6 run as the published run.** Its raw output is the
  committed real result set; the document discloses the run's incompleteness
  (67/71 sets), the noisy HTTP/2 core round-robin peaks, the rig-limited
  generator ceiling (ADR-0022), the soft Docker Desktop VM throughput ceiling,
  and the degraded-slice anomaly. This is the honest-benchmarking posture
  ADR-0022 sets.
- **The degraded slice did not isolate the slow backend in this run.** Every
  degraded run's p50 sits in the low 50s and the raw minimum latency is 50 ms,
  so the injected 50 ms delay reached all four backends rather than backend3
  alone; with no fast backend to prefer, least-connections and p2c-ewma could
  not shift away, and only consistent-hash showed the predicted split (the LB
  spilled, Nginx pinned). Reported as a limitation of the run, not a verdict on
  the algorithms. No harness change (out of scope).
- **Found a latent bug in `allowed_cells`** (`bench/generate-results.sh`,
  S5.T10.3): it does not reset `is_table`/`hdr`/`unit` at `BEGIN`/`END
  GENERATED` boundaries, so the first header line of a section after a table is
  consumed as a data row and the degraded/hot-key `(ms)` cells are never
  registered as valid narrative targets. The check silently cannot validate
  figures quoted from them. Worked around by quoting only cells the check can
  see; recorded as a **proposed follow-up ticket** in `PROGRESS.md` per
  AGENTS.md Step 2.5, not fixed here.
